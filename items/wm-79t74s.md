A promise Abhishek made in public on 2026-08-27 and has not yet acted on. It closes a gap that
Kevin Hennessy raised and Scott escalated, so two people are watching for the answer.

WHAT HAPPENED. In #data-discussion (C03JR4V1448) on 2026-08-27 23:16 IST, Kevin posted: "I
believe payments are missing from snowflake for some oliv loans. report here -- this was the
issue I briefly noted earlier @Scott Morgan", linking
web-reports.sterling.edgefocuspartners.com/perk/northpond/first_payment/nelnet-payment-feed-gap-2026-08-27.html.
Scott followed a minute later with "@Abhishek Doshi are you able to take a look?". Abhishek
said "Checking" and came back three hours later with the diagnosis.

**Abhishek has already answered the question — this item is only the follow-through he
committed to.** His reply, verbatim: "Had a look, it's a backfill gap, not something dropping
in the pipeline. We built the Nelnet file ingestion recently, and the feed only goes back to
28th July, so loans originated before that have no payment history to capture. We'd prioritised
the August backfill so everything EDGEX-related was covered and those loans reconcile exactly.
What's missing is basically loans on Oliv's Balance sheet which were originated before that.
**Will check with Oliv next week on backfilling that as well.**" That week is now, w/c
2026-08-31.

SO THE ASK IS NARROW AND ALREADY SCOPED: can Oliv supply Nelnet transaction files covering the
period before 2026-07-28, for the balance-sheet book. The EDGEX-purchased loans are fine and
reconcile exactly — this is the balance-sheet remainder only. Nothing is broken in the
pipeline; there is simply no source data to ingest for that window.

CONTEXT ON THE INGESTION ITSELF is on [[wm-gj5tkx]] — Nelnet replaced the FCC feed as Oliv's
servicer, the files carry the investor tag, and the ingestion landed through PRs #6323 / #6324
in mid-August. The 2026-07-28 floor is a property of what Oliv has sent us, not of the code.

WHO TO ASK. Nate Wong is the Oliv-side counterpart for file requests and is already the
addressee on two other outstanding file asks — [[wm-hbujve]] (the remaining 2026-08-04 files)
and [[wm-v7apt2]] (the ANL question). Bundling this with those is likely cheaper than a third
separate message, and the Oliv group DM (C0BJ1M304BU) is where the other two are meant to go.
