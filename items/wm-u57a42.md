   wrote its rows and then failed on `consume_stream`:
   `Object 'DEV_ABHISHEK.SILVER.API_CREDIT_ATTRIBUTES_STREAM' does not exist`.
   The stream is declared in `terraform/snowflake/silver_api_credit_attributes.tf`
   and does exist in PROD (created 2026-06-25, owner `PROD_WRITER`). Because it
   sits on the **shared** target table, the likely reading is DEV terraform drift
   that would hit sofi/prosper/upgrade/anchored/openroad identically — but that

   trying any other platform's credit-attributes asset. If it really is drift,

   `s3://efp-sandbox/abhishek/dev-1498/` and read back with pandas for the parity
   diff. They were never ingested through `ingest_prediction_files` →
   `s3_prediction_files` → `s3_predictions` → `silver.predictions`. Nothing yet
   proves the rows land in `silver.predictions` with the right schema, or that
   `predicted_cashflows` consumes them. This is the biggest untested seam in the
   PR and the one most likely to hide a surprise.

Until 1–3 are done the PR stays draft — its Testing section overstates coverage
by saying "validated in DEV_ABHISHEK" without these caveats, so the body needs a
