Two wrong facts were recorded on wm-hbujve across two sessions and Abhishek had
to correct both on 2026-08-20.

1. A parallel session logged Nate's "these are FCC(?) files" reply as him
   *questioning ownership*, needing an answer from our side. Wrong. Nate knows
   these are FCC files; it was discussed on the huddle. The question mark in the
   original log was the agent's uncertainty, and a later session (mine) read it
   as Nate's, then propagated it into ~/notes as an "unresolved ownership
   caveat".

2. I then assumed Nate would offer the copy-paste fix again, because that is
   what he did for issuance on 2026-08-13, and framed the next step as "hold the
   line against it". He never proposed it — he is checking whether he can share
   the actual 2026-08-04 files.

Both are the same failure: treating a plausible inference from prior behaviour
as recorded fact, without marking it as inference. Compounded by a second agent
reading the first agent's hedge as the counterparty's words.

Lesson for agents writing these logs: when the human was on a call the agent was
not on, the agent's reconstruction of the counterparty's position is inference.
Mark it as such, or ask, rather than logging it flat. Slack transcripts and AI
huddle notes are partial — what got settled verbally often is not in them.

Corrected in the item's log, and in
~/notes/areas/efp/platforms/northpond/feeds-and-columns.md + meta.md, which had
already been pushed to the dpx mirror carrying the wrong claim (re-pushed).
